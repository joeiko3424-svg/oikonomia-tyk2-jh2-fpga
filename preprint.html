Deterministic Sub-100 us Bare-Metal FPGA Spatial Voxel Screening and Dual-Target Structural Discovery of Allosteric TYK2 JH2 Inhibitors Selective Over JAK2

Authors: Joseph Iko1
Affiliations: 1Oikonomia BioPharma / Oikonomia Hardware Architecture Lab, New Jersey, USA
Subject Area: Bioinformatics / Biophysics and Computational Biology

Abstract

Selective allosteric inhibition of the tyrosine kinase 2 pseudokinase domain (TYK2 JH2) over the catalytic Janus kinase 2 domain (JAK2 JH1) avoids dose-limiting hematologic toxicities while treating autoimmune pathology. Here, we couple OIK-PAS-V1 (agfi-0b55f06c15a385187), a bare-metal Xilinx UltraScale+ FPGA spatial voxel engine (0000:34:00.0 on AWS EC2 f2.6xlarge) operating with a constant O(1) 0.25 KB on-chip state footprint, to a dual-target structural biology workflow contrasting human TYK2 JH2 (PDB: 6NZP) against off-target JAK2 JH1 (PDB: 4IVA, 1.887 Å binding-site Cα RMSD). Streaming 385 qualified 4,096-byte (16 × 16 × 16) 3D pharmacophore tensors across direct userspace PCIe Gen3 x16 BAR0 MMIO via native C (liboikonomia_voxel_dma.so) achieved 87.03 μs P50 (103.00 μs P99) latency with 100% bit-exact register determinism. Control benchmarking separated known JH2 binders from clinical decoys at 0.867 ROC-AUC. Multi-seed AutoDock Vina consensus docking (seeds 42, 137, 2026) identified scaffold-hopped ChEMBL 34 leads including CHEMBL6914 (ΔGTYK2 = −10.72 ± 0.04 kcal/mol, ΔΔGTYK2−JAK2 = −1.11 kcal/mol, Val690 = 3.34 Å, Lys642 = 2.86 Å) and CHEMBL266324 (ΔGTYK2 = −10.11 ± 0.12 kcal/mol, ΔΔGTYK2−JAK2 = −1.79 kcal/mol, QED = 0.654, LLE = 3.65, Val690 = 3.42 Å), alongside the oral-window analog OIK-TYK2-003 (MW = 421.46 Da, cLogP = 2.34, LLE = 4.14, ΔGTYK2 = −8.84 ± 0.02 kcal/mol).
