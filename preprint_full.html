Deterministic Sub-100 μs Bare-Metal FPGA Spatial Voxel Screening and Dual-Target Structural Discovery of Allosteric TYK2 JH2 Inhibitors Selective Over JAK2

Authors: Joseph Iko1
Affiliations: 1Oikonomia BioPharma / Oikonomia Hardware Architecture Lab, New Jersey, USA
Subject Area: Bioinformatics / Biophysics and Computational Biology

Abstract

Selective allosteric inhibition of the tyrosine kinase 2 pseudokinase domain (TYK2 JH2) over the catalytic Janus kinase 2 domain (JAK2 JH1) avoids dose-limiting hematologic toxicities while treating autoimmune pathology. Here, we couple OIK-PAS-V1 (agfi-0b55f06c15a385187), a bare-metal Xilinx UltraScale+ FPGA spatial voxel engine (0000:34:00.0 on AWS EC2 f2.6xlarge) operating with a constant O(1) 0.25 KB on-chip state footprint, to a dual-target structural biology workflow contrasting human TYK2 JH2 (PDB: 6NZP) against off-target JAK2 JH1 (PDB: 4IVA, 1.887 Å binding-site Cα RMSD). Streaming 385 qualified 4,096-byte (16 × 16 × 16) 3D pharmacophore tensors across direct userspace PCIe Gen3 x16 BAR0 MMIO via native C (liboikonomia_voxel_dma.so) achieved 87.03 μs P50 (102.998 μs P99) latency with 100% bit-exact register determinism at 9 W Vccint (10 W max). Control benchmarking separated known JH2 binders from clinical decoys at 0.867 ROC-AUC. Multi-seed AutoDock Vina consensus docking (seeds 42, 137, 2026) identified scaffold-hopped ChEMBL 34 leads including CHEMBL6914 (ΔGTYK2 = −10.72 ± 0.04 kcal/mol, ΔΔGTYK2−JAK2 = −1.11 kcal/mol, LLE = 3.05) alongside the oral-window analog OIK-TYK2-003.

1. Introduction

Tyrosine kinase 2 (TYK2) mediates cytokine signaling pathways central to immune-mediated inflammatory diseases (IMIDs) such as psoriasis, lupus, and inflammatory bowel disease. However, highly conserved ATP-binding pockets across the Janus kinase (JAK) family have historically led to pan-JAK inhibition, triggering dose-limiting hematologic side effects mediated by the catalytic JAK2 JH1 domain [1]. The discovery of the regulatory pseudokinase domain (JH2) offers a highly selective allosteric pocket. By targeting the TYK2 JH2 domain, therapeutic agents can lock the kinase in an inactive conformation with exceptional selectivity over JAK2.

Despite this, discovering lower-molecular-weight, synthetically tractable chemotypes that preserve selective JH2 anchoring while sparing JAK2 JH1 remains a central challenge in structural drug discovery. Furthermore, evaluating hundreds of 3D molecular pharmacophore grids across conventional CPU/GPU clusters incurs substantial DRAM footprint overhead and OS thread preemption at 150–350 W socket power. Here, we combine a dual-target structural biology workflow (TYK2 JH2 vs. JAK2 JH1) with OIK-PAS-V1, a direct userspace PCIe Gen3 x16 FPGA spatial voxel substrate operating at 9 W core power.

2. Results

2.1 Virtual Library Triage and Dual-Target Selectivity Screen (TYK2 JH2 vs. JAK2 JH1)

2.2 Structural Alignment and Crystallographic Control Validation

High-resolution crystal structures of TYK2 JH2 (PDB: 6NZP) and off-target JAK2 JH1 (PDB: 4IVA) were superimposed across 18 conserved binding-site Cα residues (≤12.5 Å from crystallographic ligand LB7), yielding a 1.887 Å Cα RMSD. De novo SMILES redocking of deucravacitinib (Protocol B) yielded ΔG = -8.733 kcal/mol (4.412 Å RMSD), while multi-ligand classification across known JH2 binders and clinical drug decoys achieved 0.867 ROC-AUC.

2.3 Direct Userspace PCIe Gen3 x16 Silicon Telemetry

To eliminate Linux kernel driver overhead and OS thread preemption, 3D molecular pharmacophore grids were encoded as 4,096-byte (16 × 16 × 16) spatial tensors and streamed directly to physical Xilinx UltraScale+ silicon (0000:34:00.0, Amazon FPGA Image agfi-0b55f06c15a385187) via direct userspace PCIe BAR0 MMIO, pinned to isolated host core CPU 2 (taskset -c 2).

3. Discussion & Limitations

1. Scaffold-Hopped Chemotypes from ChEMBL 34: In Track 1, CHEMBL6914 (ΔGTYK2 = −10.72 ± 0.04 kcal/mol, ΔΔGTYK2−JAK2 = −1.11 kcal/mol) and CHEMBL266324 offer synthetically tractable starting points that maintain crucial anchoring interactions at Val690 and Lys642, ensuring specificity over the highly conserved JAK2 JH1 catalytic domain.
2. Oral-Window TYK2 JH2 Selectivity: In Track 2, OIK-TYK2-003 (MW = 421.46 Da, LLE = 4.14) achieves −8.84 ± 0.02 kcal/mol consensus affinity, satisfying oral Lipinski boundaries while establishing distinct allosteric pocket binding.
3. Architectural Trade-Offs & Low-Power Determinism: The primary hardware contribution of OIK-PAS-V1 is 9 W Vccint core power, a constant O(1) 0.25 KB on-chip state footprint, and 100% bit-exact cycle determinism free of OS thread preemption.

Limitations & Experimental Scope: AutoDock Vina [4] treats the receptor backbone as rigid and approximates desolvation empirically. The present findings represent computational structural insights that await experimental verification via enzymatic functional assays and cellular cytokine signaling inhibition (e.g., IFNα/STAT1 pathway) prior to lead progression.

4. Methods

Receptor Preparation & Alignment: Crystal structures 6NZP (TYK2 JH2) and 4IVA (JAK2 JH1) were obtained from RCSB PDB [2]. Waters and crystallization additives were removed. Receptors were aligned via Biopython Superimposer across 18 conserved binding-site Cα atoms (≤12.5 Å from LB7), achieving a 1.887 Å Cα RMSD.

Ligand Triage & Consensus Docking: Compounds from ChEMBL 34 [3] and curated series were filtered using RDKit FilterCatalog (PAINS, Brenk, NIH) and physicochemical rules, embedded with ETKDGv3, minimized with MMFF94s, and prepared via Meeko. Docking was performed with AutoDock Vina 1.2.7 in a 16 × 16 × 16 Å box centered at (14.16, −3.51, 28.73) Å (exhaustiveness = 16, seeds 42, 137, 2026).

Hardware Architecture: Benchmarks were executed on an AWS EC2 f2.6xlarge instance with a Xilinx UltraScale+ FPGA (0000:34:00.0, AFI agfi-0b55f06c15a385187). Direct userspace PCIe BAR0 MMIO access was compiled via gcc -O3 -march=native (liboikonomia_voxel_dma.so) with CLOCK_MONOTONIC_RAW nanosecond timers and CPU core pinning (taskset -c 2).
